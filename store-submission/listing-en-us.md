## Description

Stop checking the same noisy feeds every morning.

Byte Sized Daily Brief finds five software-engineering articles worth your attention, explains why each one matters, and then deliberately ends. It is built for busy software engineers and technical leads who want useful signal without an endless news feed.

Each weekday edition includes:

- Five current engineering stories selected for technical depth, practical relevance, or useful disagreement.
- A concise “Why it matters” note for every selection.
- One optional evergreen engineering article that remains useful beyond the daily news cycle.
- Direct links to the original publishers.

Byte Sized checks public RSS feeds and the official Hacker News API. It does not scrape or reproduce article bodies. There is no account to create, no social feed to maintain, and no algorithm designed to keep you scrolling.

Open the app, scan the five ideas, read what earns your attention, and get back to building.
