## Notes for certification

Byte Sized Daily Brief is a hosted Progressive Web App at:
https://vyppi.github.io/byte-sized-daily-brief/

No test account is required. The app has no login, payments, subscriptions, advertising, or restricted capabilities.

The home screen displays five software-engineering links and one optional evergreen recommendation. Article links open the original publisher in a separate browser context. Headlines and metadata are sourced from public RSS feeds and the official Hacker News API; article bodies are not scraped or reproduced.

An internet connection is required to refresh the daily edition. The application shell is cached for offline startup. The privacy policy is available at:
https://vyppi.github.io/byte-sized-daily-brief/privacy/
