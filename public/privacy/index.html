Privacy,
byte sized.

Byte Sized has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email address.

What is measured

We use Azure Application Insights to measure anonymous product events such as edition views, article opens, engaged time and approximate scroll depth. Events include a random browser identifier, article ID, source, rank and edition date.

What is not collected

We do not collect article contents, full outbound URLs, search text, names, email addresses or session recordings. Browser analytics is disabled when Do Not Track is enabled.

Opt out

Run localStorage.setItem('byte-sized-analytics-opt-out', 'true') in your browser console and reload the page. Remove that value to opt back in.

Retention

Telemetry is retained for 30 days to understand whether the brief is useful and where readers leave.
